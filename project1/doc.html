3. Implementation Notes & Challenges

Challenging Parts:

  • Text-Wrapping around Transparent PNGs: Configuring precise text wraps around the Outback and Subaru 360 images required balancing float properties and margins so the body text wrapped tightly without overlapping text containers.
  • Brown Sidebar Typography: Fine-tuning contrast, sans-serif font weights, and internal padding within the brown contact box to match print contrast standards while preserving proper HTML block alignment. Still is not correct but I tried.

Ways Went 'Above and Beyond':

  • Converted and optimized all image assets to PNGs to support clean alpha transparency for floating article images.

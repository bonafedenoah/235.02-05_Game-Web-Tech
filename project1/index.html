Graveyard Keeper 2

Reviews and Gameplay

The Graveyard

The part of the game that is in the name the Graveyard

In the graveyard you will get corpses to bury and manage. Before you bury a corpse you need to prepare it properly, a corpes will have red and white Skulls attached to it. The red skull will hurt your graveyard quality and you must remove it before burying the corpse. The more white skulls you have the higher the quality of your graveyard will be. This is because they can have higher and higher quality gravestones on them.

Reviews

From Gamers

Here are the Top Ratings for Graveyard Keeper 2

I can’t wait to spend more time with Graveyard Keeper 2. It truly feels like a natural continuation of the original game with additional mechanics (I didn’t even mention the battle events!) alongside familiar ones. Make sure you say hi to Larry the Skull and the Donkey too! A beautiful life sim game with an insane amount of depth, it couldn’t be anything less than the Thumb Culture Platinum Award. Source: Thumb Culture or MobyGames.

Graveyard Keeper 2 is larger, funnier, deeper, and more confident than its predecessor. It transforms a cult management game into one of the most distinctive and absorbing simulations available. Whether you are restoring the cemetery, exploiting your undead workforce, or marching a zombie army into battle, every part of the experience delivers something memorable. Source: The Outerhaven or MobyGames.

Here are the Lower Ratings for Graveyard Keeper 2

Takes a long time to understand many of its systems. There are some troublesome bugs and the absence of an overriding threat or deadline makes it less engrossing than it could be. Source: Metro.co.uk or MobyGames.

Graveyard Keeper II is a true successor to the 2018 game. It has its areas for improvement, such as the many missions, the confusing point systems, and the lack of explanation for a long list of gameplay items. The game, however, has a lot of good, including the great music, stunning art, fun dialogue, and humor. This is a great cozy game for someone who enjoys the grind of cozy games. I would have liked to see a little more done with the storyline, but overall this game has earned itself a solid 7 out of 10. Source: Cinelinx or MobyGames.

World Map and Story

The Map and Story of the game is large

In the story of the game the world has been overrun by zombies. You and everyone else lives on the outskirts of the overrun villages. You must fight the zombies to take back the village. As you progress through the game, you will fight larger and larger zombie hordes. You must save the world and rebuild the village.

Zombies

The most unique part of the game is raising the Dead.

The is right you raise the dead as zombies to fight and craft for you. At the beginning of the game, you start with a small group of zombies, but as you progress, you can raise more and more powerful zombies to aid you. Zombies that you have craft can gain skills in the craft you have them doing, this allows you to buy perks for them. The more red skulls a zombie has the more perks it can get.

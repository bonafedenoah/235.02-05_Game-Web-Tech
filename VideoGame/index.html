Description

Marvel's Wolverine is a single-player action-adventure game in which you play as Logan, a legendary mutant equipped with razor-sharp adamantium claws and an accelerated healing factor. Stopping you from achieving your goals are ruthless mercenaries and deadly forces, like Sabretooth and The Reavers, who will continually hunt you down. The primary weapons you carry are your adamantium claws and superhuman senses, which you can use to slice and brutalize enemies until none are left standing. [...]
